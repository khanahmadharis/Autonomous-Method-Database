Every test on a medicine, run as one self-driving system.

An analytical method is

The recipe for measuring one quality of a medicine, such as its purity or how much of it has clumped together. Every batch is released on the strength of these results.

A centralized method DB is

One home for everything about every method: why it exists, its parameters, versions, experiments, risks, results and where it runs, readable by people and by machines.

From a question to a routine test, and back again

A method is never finished. It is designed, proven, used every day, watched, and changed. Pick a step to see how it runs when the data runs itself.

The method DB inside the enterprise data warehouse

Five layers. The lifecycle steps on top talk to one method DB. The method DB is a zone inside the enterprise data warehouse, beneath a filter by site and division. Every system below feeds the warehouse, and the labs sit at the bottom. Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, pick a step or a system to light up its route.
