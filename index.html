Every test on a medicine, run as one self-driving system.

An analytical method is

The recipe for measuring one quality of a medicine, such as its purity or how much of it has clumped together. Every batch is released on the strength of these results.

A centralized method DB is

One home for everything about every method: why it exists, its parameters, versions, experiments, risks, results and where it runs, readable by people and by machines.

From a question to a routine test, and back again

A method is never finished. It is designed, proven, used every day, watched, and changed. Pick a step to see what happens there today and what it looks like when the data runs itself. Switch views with the control at the top.
